## 🔬 About Me

I am a **Medical AI & Deep Learning Engineer** at **LTIM** (*Laboratoire de Technologie et Imagerie Médicale*, LR12ES06, Faculté de Médecine de Monastir, Tunisia), working under the academic supervision of **Pr. Asma Ben Abdallah**.

My research and engineering focus on bridging advanced artificial intelligence with clinical radiology and biomechanics to solve high-impact medical challenges:
- 🧠 **Spinal Cord & Brain MRI Segmentation**: Deep learning architectures (nested U-Nets, attention mechanisms) for Multiple Sclerosis (MS/SEP) lesion and tissue quantification.
- 🦴 **CT-based Biomechanical FEM Modeling**: Automated 3D finite element analysis (FEA) from clinical CT scans for spinal & femoral fracture risk estimation.
- 💬 **Clinical Agentic AI & RAG**: Specialized conversational agents based on international clinical consensus (McDonald, MAGNIMS) for diagnostic assistance.
